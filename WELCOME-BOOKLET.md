## 12. Things to See & Do

- **Chester Cathedral** — 5 min walk, free entry (donation welcome).
- **The Roman Walls** — walk the full 2-mile loop in about an hour.
- **Eastgate Clock** — said to be the second-most photographed clock in England after Big Ben.
- **Chester Rows** — unique medieval two-tier covered walkways.
- **Storyhouse** — theatre, cinema, library and restaurant, literally around the corner on Hunter Street.
- **River Dee & The Groves** — 15 min walk; rowing boats and riverside walks in summer.
- **Chester Racecourse (The Roodee)** — oldest racecourse still in use in Britain; check fixtures.
- **Chester Zoo** — UK's largest zoo, 15 min by car or 20 min on the no. 1 bus.
- **Grosvenor Museum** — free Roman and local history museum.
